One of the most powerful features of AI Admin Assistance is the ability for plugin and theme authors to hook directly in and create their own guidance and workflows. This way you can provide info, step-by-step tutorials, support and any other form of guidance you wish directly on your plugin or theme's settings, configuration and/or other admin pages.

This can help you drastically reduce the amount of support queries you receive and have to respond to, as you can now provide all the info and tutorials, and answer the most frequent questions your users have, directly in your plugin or theme's admin panel.

What's more, as soon as you create any guidance item or workflow that targets your plugin or theme's admin screens, the AI Admin Assistance help button and slide-in help panel will automatically be enabled there!

To see exactly how you can integrate your own help content into the AI Admin Assistance plugin, click the link below. It's much easier and quicker than you might think!
